RIYADVI · PRACTICAL FIELD GUIDE 01

From Idea to Impact

A practical guide to planning a software project with clarity.

Use this guide to prepare for discovery, align stakeholders and make the first release useful. You do not need every answer before starting—just enough shared context to make good decisions.

1. Define the business problem

  • What is difficult or costly today?
  • Who experiences the problem most often?
  • What happens if nothing changes?

2. Describe the desired outcome

  • What should become faster, clearer, safer or easier?
  • How will the team measure improvement?
  • What is a realistic first success milestone?

3. Understand the users and workflow

  • List the main user groups and their goals.
  • Sketch the current journey and the points where it breaks.
  • Identify accessibility, device, language and operational constraints.

4. Map systems and integrations

  • Which tools, databases, payment systems or third-party services must connect?
  • What information is sensitive and who should access it?
  • What existing data needs migration or cleanup?

5. Prioritize the first release

  • Separate must-have workflows from nice-to-have features.
  • Write acceptance criteria in plain language.
  • Record assumptions and risks that need early validation.

6. Plan delivery and feedback

  • Agree on owners, decision-makers and review cadence.
  • Break delivery into small, testable milestones.
  • Plan accessibility, security, analytics, backups and ongoing maintenance.
Discovery checklist: problem statement · primary users · desired outcome · constraints · integrations · first-release scope · success measures · decision owner.

Questions to bring to a consultation

  1. What is the most important problem to solve first?
  2. What does a successful first release look like?
  3. What can we learn before committing to the full build?
  4. What will it take to support the product after launch?
